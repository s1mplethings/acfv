# Identity Fusion

Visual identity asks who is visible; voice identity asks who sounds like the active speaker. They are deliberately independent. A strong visual span is the primary visual prior, the Recording Creator is a strong contextual prior, and voice is supporting confirmation or a conflict detector. Visual presence never proves active speech. Agreement can confirm, matching visual plus Recording prior can be probable when voice is unknown, and any credible cross-source disagreement is conflict and forces Generic ASR. Human confirmation has the highest priority. Collab and unknown guests remain explicit rather than being forced onto the primary Creator.

Avatar labels require an explicit, versioned mapping to Creator IDs. Unknown labels are retained as raw observations with no Creator assignment. Single frames are aggregated into multi-frame spans before they can contribute strong evidence.
