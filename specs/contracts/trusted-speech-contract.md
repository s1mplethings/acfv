# Trusted Speech Contract

Human-confirmed speech has priority. Trusted pseudo speech requires compatible profile version, clean overlap/music state, no critical flags, high identity/ASR/redecode confidence, and independent evidence. Adaptive corrections cannot self-promote without independent evidence. Lexicon candidates require trusted provenance. Only a human review confirming both speaker and transcript upgrades pseudo truth. Every unit points to its Creator, Recording, time span, provider/config provenance, and profile version.
