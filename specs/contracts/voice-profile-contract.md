# Voice Profile Contract

Speaker embeddings answer WHO, never WHAT. Similarity scores must not be labelled probability unless calibrated with independent holdout data. Profiles need multiple clean segments and temporal regions.

Voice remains a supporting confirmation and conflict signal. Thresholds are Creator-specific and precision-first; they must not be lowered merely to improve recall. Build regions and holdout regions cannot overlap. A provider-changing profile version must not silently reuse incompatible embeddings, trusted speech, lexicon entries, or acoustic exemplars.
